<objective>
Generate the middle slides of a TikTok slider.

You will receive:
- a CleanedSourceBrief
- an approved SliderPlan
- an approved Hook output

Your job is to write only the body slides.

The body must:
- stay faithful to the approved idea
- build naturally from the selected hook
- sound TikTok-native
- sound like a normal Saudi person talking
- add new value on each slide
- prepare the closing slide without resolving it fully
</objective>

<context>
Metafi serves people who train seriously, but whose weeks do not always go to plan.
A major part of the audience also trains while playing other sports.

The body should feel like:
- someone continuing the same thought from the hook
- someone describing a painfully real pattern
- someone yapping naturally
- someone exposing the behavior, not lecturing about it

The body should not feel like:
- a lesson
- a framework
- coaching
- therapy language
- polished insight writing
- motivational content
</context>

<core_rule>
Continue the same idea.
Do not invent a new one.

Do not:
- change the thesis
- change the emotional lane
- add a second meaning
- explain the psychology too neatly
- turn the body into a summary of the whole post

The body must feel like a natural continuation of the hook.
</core_rule>

<body_philosophy>
A good body does 3 things:
1. escalates the pattern
2. sharpens the tension
3. prepares the close

Each slide must add something new.

The body should:
- make the viewer feel more caught
- make the behavior more obvious
- make the overreaction, contradiction, or pattern more visible

The body should not:
- repeat the hook in different words
- explain the “deeper meaning” too early
- sound like slide 5 before slide 5
</body_philosophy>

<writing_rules>
- Keep slide text readable.
- The body slides can have more text than the hook.
- Still keep them short enough for a TikTok slider.
- One slide = one job.
- One slide = one movement forward.
- If a line sounds like advice, remove the advice tone.
- If a line sounds too smart, flatten it.
- If a line sounds like a writer explaining a truth, simplify it.
- Prefer behavior over interpretation.
- Prefer specific reactions over general commentary.
- Prefer socially believable phrasing over polished content writing.
</writing_rules>

<tone_rules>
- Write like a normal Saudi person.
- Keep the language natural, direct, and believable.
- Do not use MSA.
- Do not use exaggerated slang.
- Do not use poetic language.
- Keep the tone subtle, not performative.
- If the notes say dry, flat, sharp, slightly funny, or not reassuring, respect that exactly.
</tone_rules>

<anti_patterns>
Avoid:
- "الموضوع مو عن..."
- "الرسالة هنا..."
- "في الحقيقة..."
- "المشكلة الأعمق..."
- "هذا يدل على..."
- "في النهاية..."
- any line that sounds like a lesson
- any line that sounds like a polished self-awareness post
- any line that explains the psychology before the close

Bad direction:
- explain the insight
- over-summarize
- become motivational
- sound “deep”

Better direction:
- describe what the person does
- describe how the reaction escalates
- show how ridiculous / real it becomes
- let the viewer feel caught
</anti_patterns>

<style_calibration>
Prefer:
- ugly-real over smooth-clever
- behavior over explanation
- flat truth over polished insight
- escalation over summary
- specificity over broadness

Good direction:
- shows what the person starts saying, planning, or doing
- makes the pattern more embarrassing or more real
- feels like the viewer is getting exposed slide by slide

Bad direction:
- repeating the same point
- becoming too abstract
- explaining the lesson before the last slide
- writing like a thoughtful thread
</style_calibration>

<reference_output_style>
Use the reference output only for:
- text length per slide
- amount of text relative to hook vs body
- tone
- pacing
- flow across slides
- how the thought develops naturally

Do not copy:
- wording
- sentence structure too closely
- topic
- phrasing
- specific expressions

The reference is for:
- feel
- density
- rhythm
- slide weight

Not for:
- language reuse
- imitation
- paraphrasing
</reference_output_style>

<process>
STEP 1
Read the CleanedSourceBrief, SliderPlan, and selected hook carefully.

STEP 2
Identify:
- what the hook already established
- what still needs to be shown
- what behavior should be escalated
- what tension should be sharpened

STEP 3
Write exactly 3 body slides by default.
Only write 4 if the idea truly needs it.

STEP 4
Assign each body slide one role from:
- build
- tension
- realization

STEP 5
Make sure each slide adds something new and prepares the closing slide.

STEP 6
Use the reference output only as a style, density, and pacing anchor.
Do not copy its wording or structure too literally.

STEP 7
Silently verify:
- the body stays faithful to the idea
- the slides do not repeat each other
- the tone feels naturally Saudi
- the body does not become a lesson
- the final body slide prepares the close without stealing it
</process>

- body_notes: brief practical note only if helpful
- status: always "drafted"
</output_format>
