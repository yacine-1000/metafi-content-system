## Evaluation Instructions

Score the post across five dimensions. All scores must be integers within their allowed range. The total strategy_score must equal the exact sum of all five dimension scores.

Dimensions:
- core_pain_fit: 0–30. Does the post address a real changed-week, plan-mismatch, sport+gym conflict, recovery/load confusion, or uncertainty about what to train next? Generic gym motivation, bodybuilding tips, or discipline content score low.
- real_life_specificity: 0–20. Is there a specific real-life situation — not vague, not generic? Does it feel lived-in?
- metafi_differentiation: 0–20. Does the post point toward a plan that adapts — not just willpower, habits, or generic advice? Does it land on the static plan as the problem, not the person?
- tone_fit: 0–15. Is the language simple, direct, and human? Reject poetic, consultant, therapy, or hype language.
- product_restraint: 0–15. Does the post avoid hard selling or generic app advertising? Restrained product presence scores high.

Score meaning:
- 85–100: strong fit
- 70–84: usable
- 50–69: needs fix
- below 50: reject

Flags that must trigger low scores or high drift_risk:
- Generic gym motivation → low core_pain_fit, high drift_risk
- Generic fitness education → low metafi_differentiation, high drift_risk
- Bodybuilding tips → reject drift
- Discipline or productivity content → reject drift
- Hard product pitch → low product_restraint
- Content with no changed-week, no plan mismatch, no sport+gym conflict, no recovery/load confusion, and no training uncertainty → high drift_risk

---

## Output Format

Return a single JSON object only. No markdown. No explanation outside the JSON.

Required fields:
- strategy_score: integer, sum of all five dimension scores
- score_breakdown: object with core_pain_fit, real_life_specificity, metafi_differentiation, tone_fit, product_restraint
- drift_risk: "low", "medium", or "high"
- metafi_fit: "strong", "usable", "needs_fix", or "reject"
- primary_pain: short string describing the core pain the post is targeting
- fix_required: true if metafi_fit is "needs_fix" or "reject", otherwise false
- issues: array of strings, each describing a specific problem found (empty array if none)
- improvement_suggestions: array of strings, each a concrete suggestion (empty array if none)
- reasoning_summary: one short paragraph explaining the overall evaluation
- status: "checked"
