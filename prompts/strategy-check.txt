## Evaluation Instructions

### Step 1: Classify content_type

Choose exactly one:
- direct_metafi — explicitly about adaptive training, changed weeks, plan mismatch, or uncertainty about what to train next.
- sports_gym — sport + gym overlap: football, padel, tennis, running, soreness, fatigue, recovery, and how sport activity affects gym training.
- gym_life_relatable — relatable gym moments: awkward situations, inconsistency, confusion, low energy, feeling off, embarrassment, or routine friction.
- general_fitness — useful training, recovery, consistency, or gym habit content. Practical and connected to training reality.
- off_brand — unrelated lifestyle, fake motivation, hard product pitch, generic "buy our app," empty hype, or content with no useful training angle.

### Step 2: Classify content_relationship

How closely does this post relate to Metafi's core message of adaptive training?

Choose exactly one:
- direct — explicitly about adaptive training, plan mismatch, or uncertainty about what to train next.
- adjacent — sport/gym overlap, recovery, or training context awareness. Close to the core but not explicit.
- broad — relatable gym moment, general fitness, or useful training content. Within Metafi's content world but not product-specific.
- off_world — unrelated to training reality. No useful angle for Metafi's audience.

### Step 3: Determine allowed

- allowed = true for: direct_metafi, sports_gym, gym_life_relatable, general_fitness (when useful, relatable, and training-related).
- allowed = false only for: off_brand, content that is uselessly generic, robotic, hollow, a hard product pitch, or unsafe/reckless fitness advice.
- A post does NOT need to mention Metafi or adaptive planning to be allowed.
- Do not mark general_fitness as disallowed simply because it is broad. Judge usefulness and relatability.
- Do not penalize posts for being adjacent or broad if they genuinely serve a training audience.

### Step 4: Score the post

Score across five dimensions. All scores must be integers within their allowed range. strategy_score must equal the exact sum of all five scores.

Dimensions:
- core_pain_fit: 0–30. Does the post address a real training friction, conflict, or uncertainty? Off_brand and hollow motivation score low. Relatable gym life and sport conflicts can score well.
- real_life_specificity: 0–20. Is there a specific, lived-in situation — not vague or generic?
- metafi_differentiation: 0–20. Does the post point toward adaptive thinking, plan flexibility, or awareness of training context? It does not need to name Metafi. Relatable gym or sport content can score mid-range here.
- tone_fit: 0–15. Is the language simple, direct, and human? Reject poetic, consultant, therapy, or hype language.
- product_restraint: 0–15. Does the post avoid hard selling or generic app advertising? Restrained or absent product presence scores high.

A moderate total score (50–69) does not automatically mean reject if allowed = true. Judge whether the content serves the audience.

Score meaning:
- 85–100: strong fit
- 70–84: usable
- 50–69: needs human review
- below 50: reject

### Step 5: Set fix_required

fix_required = true when:
- allowed = false, OR
- metafi_fit is "needs_fix" or "reject"

fix_required = false otherwise.

fix_required means a human should review or fix the post — it does NOT mean the post must be forced into Metafi's core message. Broad and adjacent posts can be allowed without fixing.

### Step 6: Write advisory_notes

List any specific observations that a human editor should know. These are advisory, not blocking.

Examples of good advisory notes:
- "Post is broad but clean — no fix needed, just awareness."
- "Caption could be more specific to the situation."
- "Hook is strong but slightly vague on the sport."
- "Score is moderate; content serves the audience well despite not referencing adaptive planning."

Leave the array empty if there is nothing notable to flag.

## Output Format

Return a single JSON object only. No markdown. No explanation outside the JSON.

Required fields:
- content_type: one of direct_metafi, sports_gym, gym_life_relatable, general_fitness, off_brand
- content_relationship: one of direct, adjacent, broad, off_world
- allowed: boolean
- strategy_score: integer, sum of all five dimension scores
- score_breakdown: object with core_pain_fit, real_life_specificity, metafi_differentiation, tone_fit, product_restraint
- drift_risk: "low", "medium", or "high"
- metafi_fit: "strong", "usable", "needs_fix", or "reject"
- primary_pain: short string describing the core pain or topic the post is targeting
- fix_required: true if allowed=false or metafi_fit is needs_fix/reject, otherwise false
- issues: array of strings, each describing a specific problem found (empty array if none)
- improvement_suggestions: array of strings, each a concrete suggestion (empty array if none)
- advisory_notes: array of strings, each a soft observation for a human editor (empty array if none)
- reasoning_summary: one short paragraph explaining the overall evaluation
- status: "checked"
