## Your Task

Read the source notes carefully. Choose the most natural angle for this content.

**Do not force every post into Metafi's core message.**
If the source is stronger as a relatable gym moment, plan it as a relatable gym moment.
If it fits general fitness, plan it as general fitness.
If it genuinely fits adaptive training / plan mismatch, plan it as direct Metafi.

Follow what the source actually contains — not what would be most convenient for the app.

### Allowed Angles

- **direct**: The source is explicitly about adaptive training, plan mismatch, or uncertainty about what to train next. Metafi's core territory.
- **adjacent**: Sport/gym overlap — soreness, fatigue, recovery, sport affecting gym training. Close to Metafi but not explicit.
- **broad**: Relatable gym moment, consistency, routine friction, general fitness habit. Useful and relatable but not product-specific.
- **off_world**: Unrelated lifestyle, empty motivation, no training angle. Do not plan this — flag it and stop.

Use the `do_not_force` list from source notes. If a direction appears there, do not choose it.

### Content Rules

- Choose one specific situation. Do not combine multiple scenarios.
- Preserve natural source texture — specific phrases, concrete details, the human voice.
- Do not write "or" chains like "نوم أو سفر أو كورة."
- Do not use poetic, consultant, coach, or motivational language.
- Do not pitch the product directly. Do not write "Metafi solves this."
- Write planning fields in clear, plain language — complete thoughts, not slogans.
- The post should feel like a normal person describing something that actually happened.

### Forbidden concepts

Do not use: false choice, reframe, insight, transformation, framework, strategy, "الحل", "السر", "غير حياتك", "ابدأ الآن".

Do not mention Metafi or متعافي in planning fields unless the angle is direct and the source explicitly mentions the product.

---

## Slide Structure

Plan exactly 5 slides. Assign a role to each slide.

Slide roles are flexible — choose what fits this specific content. Common roles include: hook, tension, conflict, observation, realization, shift, resolution, close, product_mention. Use what makes sense. Do not force a rigid formula.

---

## Output Format

Return a single JSON object only. No markdown. No explanation outside the JSON.

Required fields:

- slider_plan_id: short unique string, e.g. "plan-001"
- chosen_angle: one of "direct", "adjacent", "broad", "off_world"
- why_this_angle: one plain sentence explaining why this angle fits the source
- content_relationship: one of "direct", "adjacent", "broad", "off_world" — same value as chosen_angle
- human_truth: one complete sentence about what is real and relatable in this source
- texture_to_preserve: array of specific phrases, details, or tone elements from the source worth keeping
- what_not_to_force: array of directions or framings to avoid in later phases
- slide_count: must be 5
- slide_roles: array of exactly 5 objects, each with:
  - slide_number: 1 through 5
  - role: string label for this slide's function
  - purpose: one sentence describing what this slide does
  - key_point: the specific idea or moment this slide should land
- tone_direction: one short plain sentence describing the overall tone for copy phases
- status: must be exactly "planned"
