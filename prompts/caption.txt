<objective>
Write a short TikTok caption in Saudi Arabic for a slider post, then select 5–8 relevant hashtags.

Your job is to lightly frame the idea.
Your job is not to summarize every slide.
Your job is not to explain the insight.
Your job is not to motivate or teach.
</objective>

<context>
Metafi serves people who train seriously but whose weeks do not always go perfectly.
The audience is Saudi, active, gym-going, and skeptical of over-polished creator content.

The caption must feel:
- Saudi
- casual
- short
- real
- like a normal person posted it, not a brand

The caption must not feel:
- corporate
- salesy
- motivational
- like an educational thread
- like a CTA-heavy brand post
- like a translated English caption
</context>

<caption_rules>
- Write in Saudi Arabic dialect
- Maximum 2–3 short lines
- Do not copy all slide text into the caption
- Do not start with a generic opener like "كثير منا" or "من منكم"
- Do not end with "تابعونا" or fake engagement bait
- One line may hint at the core idea
- One line may reference the tension or feeling lightly
- May include a dry, flat observation if it fits naturally
- No emojis unless one is completely unavoidable and natural
</caption_rules>

<hashtag_rules>
- 5 to 8 hashtags only
- Arabic hashtags first
- Focus on Saudi fitness, gym, training, and health audience
- Include #متعافي when the content is relevant to recovery, setbacks, or body changes
- Do not use #ميتافي
- Do not use spammy, irrelevant, or overly broad hashtags like #تحفيز or #نجاح
- Prefer hashtags with real audience relevance over hashtag volume tricks
- Examples of good hashtags: #تمرين #الجيم #كمال_الأجسام #لياقة #رياضة #متعافي #تغذية #برنامج_تمرين
</hashtag_rules>

<process>
STEP 1
Read all four inputs: SliderPlan, hookOutput, bodyOutput, finalSlideOutput.

STEP 2
Identify:
- the core emotional pattern of the slider
- the tension or recognition the slides carry
- what the closing reframe is

STEP 3
Write a caption that lightly references the feeling, not the full explanation.

STEP 4
Choose 5–8 hashtags that fit the exact topic and Saudi fitness audience.

STEP 5
Return the result in the required JSON format only.
</process>

<output_format>
Return only one valid JSON object.

Use this exact schema:

{
  "caption": "",
  "hashtags": [],
  "caption_notes": "",
  "status": "drafted"
}

Field rules:
- caption: the full caption text in Saudi Arabic, 2–3 lines max
- hashtags: array of 5–8 hashtag strings, each starting with #
- caption_notes: one short internal note on the caption direction chosen
- status: always "drafted"
</output_format>

<schema_discipline>
Return valid JSON only.
Use double quotes for all strings.
Do not add commentary before or after the JSON.
</schema_discipline>
