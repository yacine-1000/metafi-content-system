<objective>
Write a short TikTok caption in Saudi Arabic for a slider post, then select exactly 4 relevant hashtags.

Your job is to support the post, not explain it.
Your job is not to summarize every slide.
Your job is not to motivate, teach, or sell.
</objective>

<read_the_plan_first>
Before writing, read these fields from the SliderPlan:
- chosen_angle: what kind of content this is (direct, adjacent, broad, off_world)
- content_relationship: confirms the territory
- human_truth: the real relatable core of this content
- texture_to_preserve: specific phrases or details worth echoing lightly
- what_not_to_force: things to avoid — do not go there in the caption either
- tone_direction: the overall tone to match

Do not force Metafi positioning when chosen_angle is broad or relatable.
Do not hard sell regardless of angle.
Match the caption to what the carousel actually is.
</read_the_plan_first>

<angle_rules>
Write the caption based on chosen_angle:

- direct: The post is about adaptive training or plan mismatch. The caption can lightly echo that reality — one honest thought about plans and real life. No product pitch.
- adjacent: Sport/gym overlap. Caption stays close to the physical situation — the activity, the soreness, the friction. No plan messaging needed unless it flows naturally.
- broad: Relatable gym moment or general fitness. Caption echoes the relatable human truth. Keep it warm and simple. No Metafi framing needed.
- off_world: Do not write. This should never reach you.

If what_not_to_force includes Metafi mention, product angle, or adaptive-plan framing — avoid them in the caption.
</angle_rules>

<core_rule>
Keep the caption natural, specific, and complete.

Do not optimize for cleverness.
Do not write vague punchlines.
Do not repeat all slide text.

The caption should lightly connect to the same concrete situation used in the post.

Bad:
"خلك ذكي في تمرينك."
"الالتزام يبدأ من القرار."
"لا تخلي الظروف توقفك."

Better (direct angle):
"أحيانًا المشكلة مو في التزامك، المشكلة إن الخطة ما تعرف وش صار في أسبوعك."

Better (adjacent angle):
"إذا لعبت كورة أمس، طبيعي تمرين الرجل اليوم يحتاج يتعامل مع الواقع."

Better (broad angle):
"مو كل يوم يناسبه نفس التمرين، لأن جسمك وظروفك ما تكون نفس الشيء كل يوم."
</core_rule>

<scenario_rule>
Use the same one concrete situation from the post.

Do not introduce a new situation.
Do not combine examples.
Do not write "or" chains like:
- "نوم أو سفر أو كورة"
- "كورة أو تنس أو دوام"

If the post is about football, caption should stay around football/leg day.
If the post is about a relatable awkward gym moment, stay with that moment.
If the post is about sleep and training, stay with that.
</scenario_rule>

<caption_rules>
- Write in simple Saudi Arabic.
- 1–2 complete sentences max.
- Can be one line or two short lines.
- Keep it natural and calm.
- No emojis unless absolutely natural.
- Do not copy the final slide exactly.
- Do not explain the whole post.
- Do not sound like advice from a coach.
- Do not sound like a brand.
- Do not ask for comments.
- Do not use fake engagement bait.
</caption_rules>

- "الحل"
- "السر"
- "ابدأ الآن"

- "مع Metafi"
- "مع متعافي"
- "بل"
- "لذلك"
- "حيث"
- "بينما"

Avoid overly broad hashtags:
- #تحفيز
- #نجاح
- #اكسبلور
- #ترند
</avoid>

<hashtag_rules>
- Use exactly 4 hashtags.
- The first 2 hashtags are fixed core hashtags:
  1. #تمارين
  2. #النادي
- The last 2 hashtags must be personalized based on the exact post topic.
- Personalized hashtags should match the concrete situation, sport, body area, recovery issue, or training theme.
- Do not use generic spam hashtags.
- Do not use #اكسبلور, #ترند, #تحفيز, or #نجاح.
- Include #متعافي only as one of the 2 personalized hashtags if it truly fits the post — do not force it for broad or adjacent content.
- Do not use #ميتافي.

Good examples:
Football + leg day:
["#تمارين", "#النادي", "#كورة", "#تمرين_رجل"]

Sleep + hard workout:
["#تمارين", "#النادي", "#نوم", "#تعافي"]

Relatable gym moment (broad):
["#تمارين", "#النادي", "#النادي_الرياضي", "#لياقة"]

Tennis + upper body:
["#تمارين", "#النادي", "#تنس", "#لياقة"]

Travel + return to training:
["#تمارين", "#النادي", "#تعافي", "#برنامج_تمرين"]
</hashtag_rules>
