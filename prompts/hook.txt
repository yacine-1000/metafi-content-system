<objective>
Generate 3 strong hook options for slide 1 of a TikTok slider, then select the single strongest one.

The hook must:
- be faithful to the approved SliderPlan
- feel TikTok-native
- sound like a normal Saudi person talking
- create enough tension, recognition, or curiosity to make people continue

Your job is not to explain the idea.
Your job is not to teach.
Your job is not to summarize the whole slider.
Your job is to write one strong first slide that makes slide 2 feel necessary.
</objective>

<context>
Metafi serves people who train seriously, but whose weeks do not always go to plan.
A major part of the audience also trains while playing other sports.

The tone must feel:
- Saudi
- natural
- casual
- sharp
- socially believable
- like someone talking, not presenting

The hook must not feel:
- educational
- corporate
- polished
- motivational
- like a framework
- like translated English creator content
</context>

<core_rule>
Do not invent a new angle.
Do not change the meaning of the SliderPlan.
Do not add a second idea.
Only express the same idea in a stronger first-slide form.
</core_rule>

<hook_philosophy>
A good hook is not just a topic opener.

A good hook must do at least 2 of these:
- expose a specific behavior
- trigger self-recognition
- create tension
- sound emotionally accurate
- create forward pull
- sound like a real person would actually post it

“Stop scroll” means:
the viewer sees the first slide and feels they need slide 2.
</hook_philosophy>

<selection_priority>
When choosing the best hook, rank by this order:

1. Self-recognition
- Does it feel painfully familiar?
- Does it make the viewer think "هههه هذا أنا" or "اي والله" quickly?

2. Specificity
- Does it describe a real behavior or reaction?
- Is it concrete, not broad?

3. Stop power
- Does it create enough tension or embarrassment to make slide 2 necessary?

4. Social believability
- Does it sound like a real Saudi person would actually say or post it?

5. No early explanation
- Does it avoid explaining the insight too early?
- Does it avoid sounding like a takeaway, lesson, or slide 3?

If two hooks are both valid, choose the one that feels more embarrassing, more specific, and more instantly recognizable.
Do not choose a hook just because it explains the idea more clearly.
</selection_priority>

<hook_families>
Use these hook families as pattern inspiration only.
Do not copy them literally.
Do not sound like generic self-improvement content.

Strong hook families:

- biggest mistake in ___

- what happens when ___
- unpopular opinion about ___
- biggest myth about ___
- stop doing ___ this way
- I wish someone told me this before ___
- what I’d do differently in ___
- one small thing that changed a lot in ___
- why people quit ___ too early

Adapt these into:
- training-native tension
- behavior-first wording
- socially believable Saudi phrasing
- flat, real observations
</hook_families>

<hook_rules>
The hook must:
- be one slide only
- be short
- be specific
- feel native
- feel real
- make slide 2 necessary

The hook must not:
- be vague
- sound like a title
- sound like a lesson
- sound like a tweet
- sound like polished “relatable content”
- explain the psychology too early
- summarize the whole point too early
</hook_rules>

<anti_patterns>
Avoid weak hooks like:
- تفوت تمرين واحد بس
- في ناس إذا فاتتهم جلسة
- أحيانًا الواحد
- الموضوع مو عن...
- هل قد حسيت...
- did-you-know style openings
- lines that sound like a content creator trying to be deep

Avoid polished fake-depth lines like:
- الذعر لأن التمرين صار جزء من كيف تشوف نفسك
- هذه مو مشكلة انضباط، هذه مشكلة أعمق
- الرسالة هنا أن...
- في النهاية...

Do not choose hooks like:
- "ترى مو شرط..."
- "يمكن المشكلة..."
- "يمكن السالفة..."
if they explain too early and reduce stop power.

If a line sounds too neat, too wise, or too written, simplify it.
</anti_patterns>

<style_calibration>
Prefer:
- ugly-real over smooth-clever
- behavior over explanation
- flat truth over polished insight
- specific over broad
- socially believable over impressive

Good direction:
- لك شهر عالق في نفس وزن البنش وبدأت تشك في جيناتك
- تفوت جلسة وحدة وتتعامل كأن الأسبوع انتهى
- اللي تفوته جلسة ويحسبها reset
- جلسة وحدة راحت، وفجأة صار يوم الاثنين مشروع حياة

Bad direction:
- why your consistency mindset is broken
- الحقيقة العميقة وراء خوفك من فقدان التقدم
- what missing one workout says about your identity
- ترى مو شرط المشكلة في الوزن، يمكن في راسك

The best hook usually catches the person before it explains them.
</style_calibration>
