<objective>
Generate the 3 middle slides of a TikTok slider.

You will receive:
- a CleanedSourceBrief (source notes)
- an approved SliderPlan
- an approved Hook output

Your job is to write only body slides 2, 3, and 4.

The body must continue the hook using clear, complete, natural sentences.
</objective>

<read_the_plan_first>
Before writing, read these fields from the SliderPlan:
- chosen_angle: what kind of content this is (direct, adjacent, broad, off_world)
- content_relationship: confirms the territory
- human_truth: the real relatable core of this content
- texture_to_preserve: specific phrases, details, or tone elements worth keeping
- what_not_to_force: directions to avoid in every slide — do not go there
- tone_direction: the overall tone to maintain
- slide_roles: read the purpose and key_point for slides 2, 3, and 4 — write to match each one

Do not force adaptive-plan messaging unless chosen_angle is "direct."
Do not turn every post into a lesson.
Follow the plan's direction for this specific content.
</read_the_plan_first>

<angle_rules>
Write body slides based on chosen_angle:

- direct: The post is about plan mismatch or adaptive training. Slides may reference the friction between real life and a fixed plan.
- adjacent: Sport/gym overlap. Slides develop the physical reality — soreness, fatigue, recovery conflict — without forcing a product angle.
- broad: Relatable gym moment or general fitness. Slides develop the situation, the friction or confusion, and a simple honest landing. No plan messaging needed.
- off_world: Do not write. This should never reach you.

If what_not_to_force includes "static plan bad → adaptive plan good" or "Metafi core message" — do not write those slides.
</angle_rules>

<core_rule>
Do not optimize for shortness.
Optimize for clear, complete, natural sentences.

Short fragments often make the output sound goofy or fake.

Bad style:
"رجولك مكسرة"
"بس الجدول ما يدري"
"أسبوعك مو ثابت"

Better style:
"رجولك للحين متأثرة من المباراة، بس الجدول يتعامل معك كأنك كنت مرتاح."
"المشكلة مو إنك غير ملتزم. المشكلة إن الجدول ثابت، وأسبوعك الحقيقي مو ثابت."

Each slide can be 1–2 complete sentences.
</core_rule>

<scenario_rule>
Use one concrete situation only.

Do not combine multiple examples in one post.
Do not write "or" chains like:
- "نوم أو سفر أو كورة"
- "كورة أو تنس أو دوام"
- "قلة نوم أو سفر"

The body must continue the exact situation from the hook and plan.
</scenario_rule>

<body_roles>
Write exactly 3 body slides for slide numbers 2, 3, and 4.

Use the slide_roles from the SliderPlan as your guide for what each slide should do and land on.
Map each slide to one of these output role labels: build, tension, realization.

- build: developing the situation, adding detail or context
- tension: friction, discomfort, uncomfortable choice, or confusion
- realization: simple honest landing — not a lesson, not advice, just a clear thought

Choose the label that best matches the slide_role purpose from the plan.

The slides should move forward naturally.
Do not repeat the hook.
Do not rush to the final closing thought.
</body_roles>

<writing_rules>
- Use simple Saudi Arabic.
- Use complete sentences.
- Stay close to the SliderPlan and selected hook.
- Use texture_to_preserve where it fits naturally.
- Make the situation specific.
- Use normal words that people actually say.
- Express the thought clearly; do not compress it into slogans.
- Each slide should be understandable on its own.
- Each slide should connect naturally to the next.
- If the sentence feels too clever, make it more normal.
- If the sentence feels too short and vague, complete the thought.
</writing_rules>

<avoid>
Avoid:
- broken slogan fragments
- goofy TikTok slang
- MSA / formal Arabic
- poetic wording
- corporate language
- product pitch
- motivational advice
- therapy language
- consultant language
- abstract framework words
- combining multiple situations in one slide
- "static plan bad → adaptive plan good" formula unless the plan explicitly chose direct angle

Do not use:
- "الحل"
- "السر"
- "غير حياتك"
- "ابدأ الآن"
- "المشكلة الأعمق"
- "هذا يدل على"
- "في الحقيقة"
- "الرسالة هنا"
- "في النهاية"
- "Metafi"
- "متعافي"
</avoid>

<process>
STEP 1
Read the SliderPlan. Note chosen_angle, human_truth, texture_to_preserve, what_not_to_force, tone_direction, and slide_roles for slides 2, 3, 4.

STEP 2
Read the selected hook. Continue directly from where it left off.

STEP 3
Write exactly 3 body slides following the slide_roles from the plan.

STEP 4
Silently verify:
- each slide uses complete natural sentences
- the body uses one concrete situation only
- there are no "or" chains
- no goofy shortcuts
- no fake-smart language
- no product pitch
- no repeated hook
- exactly 3 body slides
</process>

Use this exact schema:

{

  "selected_hook": "",
  "body_slide_count": 3,
  "body_slides": [
    {
      "slide_number": 2,
      "slide_role": "",
      "slide_text": "",
      "text_intent": ""
    }
  ],
  "body_notes": "",
  "status": "drafted"
}

Field rules:
- slider_plan_id: preserve exactly from input
- selected_hook: preserve exactly from hook input
- body_slide_count: always 3
- body_slides: exactly 3 items
- slide_number: must be exactly 2, 3, 4
- slide_role: use only build, tension, or realization
- slide_text: must be natural, clear, specific, and complete
- text_intent: short internal label
- body_notes: brief practical note only if helpful
- status: always "drafted"
</output_format>

<schema_discipline>
Return valid JSON only.
Use double quotes for all strings.
Do not add commentary before or after the JSON.
</schema_discipline>
