<objective>
Generate the final text slide of a TikTok slider.

You will receive:
- an approved SliderPlan
- an approved Hook output
- approved Body slides

Your job is to write the last text slide.

The final slide must land the idea clearly without sounding like an ad, slogan, or motivational quote.
</objective>

<read_the_plan_first>
Before writing, read these fields from the SliderPlan:
- chosen_angle: what kind of content this is (direct, adjacent, broad, off_world)
- content_relationship: confirms the territory
- human_truth: the real relatable core of this content
- texture_to_preserve: phrases or details worth keeping in the close
- what_not_to_force: directions to avoid — do not go there
- tone_direction: the overall tone to maintain
- slide_roles[4]: the role and purpose of slide 5 — write to match this

Do not force Metafi's core message unless chosen_angle is "direct."
Do not force a product pitch unless the plan explicitly calls for it.
Follow the plan's direction for this specific content.
</read_the_plan_first>

<angle_rules>
Choose a closing style based on chosen_angle:

- direct: The post is about plan mismatch or adaptive training. The final slide can land the idea that plans should adapt to real life — a calm honest thought, not a sales pitch.
- adjacent: Sport/gym overlap. Close on the physical reality or the honest friction — what the viewer takes away from the situation. No product angle needed.
- broad: Relatable gym moment or general fitness. Close with a simple human truth, a relatable observation, or a light practical question. No plan messaging needed.
- off_world: Do not write. This should never reach you.

If what_not_to_force includes product pitch, Metafi mention, or adaptive-plan framing — avoid them in the close.
</angle_rules>

<closing_styles>
The final slide should do one of these. Match the style to what the plan and content naturally call for:

1. flat_realization
A clear, calm sentence that lands the honest point. Default choice.
Use for: direct and adjacent angles, or broad content with a clear landing.

2. soft_reframe
A simple shift in how the viewer sees the situation — not a lesson, just a different way to look at it.
Use for: relatable gym-life moments, situations where the viewer might judge themselves unfairly.

3. comment_question
A practical or honest question that invites the viewer to relate — not engagement bait, not "comment below."
Use for: broad content where a natural question opens the thought further.

Default to flat_realization when unsure.
</closing_styles>

<core_rule>
Do not optimize for the shortest ending.
Optimize for a clear, complete, natural closing thought.

Bad:
"مو العكس"
"خلك أذكى"
"الجدول لازم يفهمك"

Better:
"أحيانًا التمرين الصح مو اللي كان مكتوب من البداية. التمرين الصح هو اللي يناسب جسمك اليوم."

Better:
"الخطة الجيدة ما تفترض إن كل أسبوع مثالي. الخطة الجيدة تعرف كيف تتعامل مع الأسبوع الحقيقي."

Better:
"مو كل يوم يناسبه نفس التمرين، لأن جسمك وظروفك ما تكون نفس الشيء كل يوم."
</core_rule>

<scenario_rule>
Keep the same one concrete situation from the hook and body.

Do not introduce a new situation.
Do not combine multiple examples.
Do not use "or" chains.

The final thought should come from the exact situation already shown.
</scenario_rule>

<writing_rules>
- Use simple Saudi Arabic.
- Use 1–2 complete sentences.
- Keep it natural and clear.
- Stay content-first.
- Use texture_to_preserve where it fits naturally.
- Do not sound like a slogan.
- Do not sound like a coach.
- Do not sound like a brand.
- Do not sound motivational.
- Do not explain the whole post again.
- Do not become poetic or fake-deep.
- Do not use broken sentence fragments.
- Avoid فصحى connectors like "بل", "لذلك", "حيث", "بينما".
- Prefer natural Saudi phrasing like "بس", "عشان", "لأن".
</writing_rules>

<avoid>
Avoid:
- sales CTA
- motivational advice
- fake-deep endings
- engagement farming
- product pitch
- formal Arabic
- exaggerated slang
- abstract consultant wording

Do not use:
- "بل"
- "لذلك"
- "حيث"
- "بينما"
- "الحل"
- "السر"
- "ابدأ الآن"
- "حمّل التطبيق"
- "تابعنا"
- "في النهاية"
- "الرسالة هنا"
- "عشان كذا لازم"
- "مع Metafi"
- "مع متعافي"
</avoid>

<brand_rules>
- The app image can carry the brand presence.
- brand_bridge_text should usually be empty.
- Do not force Metafi or متعافي into the final text.
- If the final slide is stronger without a brand bridge, leave brand_bridge_text empty.
- For direct angle posts, a quiet closing thought about adaptive training is enough — it does not need to name the product.
</brand_rules>

<process>
STEP 1
Read the SliderPlan. Note chosen_angle, human_truth, what_not_to_force, tone_direction, and slide_roles[4].

STEP 2
Read the hook and body. Identify the one concrete situation already shown.

STEP 3
Choose the closing style that fits: flat_realization, soft_reframe, or comment_question.

STEP 4
Write 1–2 complete natural sentences.

STEP 5
Leave brand_bridge_text empty by default.

STEP 6
Silently verify:
- the ending uses complete sentences
- it keeps the same situation
- it does not sound like an ad
- it does not sound motivational
- it does not introduce a new idea
- it lands the thought clearly
- no فصحى connectors
</process>
