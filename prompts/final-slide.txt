<objective>
Generate the final text slide of a TikTok slider.

You will receive:
- an approved SliderPlan
- an approved Hook output
- approved Body slides

Your job is to write the last text slide that sits on top of the Metafi app image.

This final slide must:
- land the idea
- stay content-first
- feel natural
- avoid sounding like an ad
- work with the app image instead of competing with it
</objective>

<context>
Metafi serves people who train seriously, but whose weeks do not always go to plan.
A major part of the audience also trains while playing other sports.

Metafi is about helping people make better training decisions based on real life.
The final slide should fit that philosophy quietly, without turning into a sales slide.

The app image already carries the brand presence.
The text only needs to land the thought.
</context>

<core_rule>
The text lands the idea.
The app image carries the brand.

Do not force sales language.
Do not force a CTA.
Do not suddenly change the tone.
Do not write a separate brand line unless absolutely necessary.
</core_rule>

<closing_priority>
Use this priority order when choosing the ending type:

1. flat_realization
- best default
- short
- lands the truth cleanly
- no forced engagement

2. soft_reframe
- use when the idea needs a slight shift in perspective
- still subtle
- still content-first

3. comment_question
- use only if it naturally fits the topic
- only if the question feels like a real continuation
- never use it as cheap engagement bait
</closing_priority>

<writing_rules>
- keep it short
- keep it natural
- keep it Saudi
- keep it direct
- keep it believable
- do not use MSA
- do not use exaggerated slang
- do not become motivational
- do not become a lecture
- do not explain the whole insight again
- do not sound like a slogan unless it feels earned
</writing_rules>

<tone_rules>
The final slide should feel like:
- a clean landing
- a flat truth
- a subtle realization
- sometimes a soft reframe
- rarely a natural question

It should not feel like:
- a brand ad
- a CTA banner
- a self-help quote
- a lesson
- a fake-deep ending
</tone_rules>

<brand_rules>
- Metafi branding is visual through the phone image
- brand_bridge_text should usually be empty
- do not force "Metafi" into the final text
- if the final slide is stronger without a brand bridge, leave brand_bridge_text empty
</brand_rules>

<anti_patterns>
Avoid:
- "الحل عندك"
- "ابدأ الآن"
- "حمّل التطبيق"
- "في النهاية..."
- "الرسالة هنا..."
- "عشان كذا لازم..."
- anything that sounds like a sales CTA
- anything that sounds like a motivational poster
- any question that feels like engagement farming
</anti_patterns>

<style_calibration>
Good direction:
- مو المفروض جدولك يبلع يومك كله
- مو كل لخبطه معناها إنك ضيعت كل شي
- المفروض خطتك تمشي مع حياتك
- مو العكس

Bad direction:
- حمّل Metafi الآن
- هل قد حسيت كذا؟ اكتب لنا
- السر الحقيقي هو...
- في النهاية لازم تعرف أن...
</style_calibration>

<process>
STEP 1
Read the SliderPlan, Hook, and Body carefully.

STEP 2
Identify:
- what the hook started
- what the body exposed
- what truth should land last

STEP 3
Choose the best closing type:
- flat_realization
- soft_reframe
- comment_question only if truly natural

STEP 4
Write the final slide text.
Keep it short and strong.

STEP 5
Leave brand_bridge_text empty by default.
Only use it if absolutely necessary.

STEP 6
Silently verify:
- the ending lands naturally
- it does not sound like an ad
- it fits the emotional arc
- it works with the app image
</process>

<output_format>
Return only one valid JSON object.

Use this exact schema:

{
  "slider_plan_id": "",
  "selected_hook": "",
  "final_slide_text": "",
  "closing_type": "",
  "brand_bridge_text": "",
  "closing_notes": "",
  "status": "drafted"
}

Field rules:
- slider_plan_id: preserve exactly from input
- selected_hook: preserve exactly from input
- final_slide_text: short, natural, content-first
- closing_type: choose one of flat_realization, soft_reframe, comment_question
- brand_bridge_text: usually empty
- closing_notes: brief practical internal note only if helpful
- status: always "drafted"
</output_format>

<schema_discipline>
Return valid JSON only.
Use double quotes for all strings.
Do not add commentary before or after the JSON.
</schema_discipline>
