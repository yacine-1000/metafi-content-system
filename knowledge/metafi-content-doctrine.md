# Metafi Content Doctrine

## 1. Core Content Promise

- A training plan that adapts to your week.
- You always know exactly what to train.

## 2. Metafi Content Territory

Content should focus on:

- Changed-week training problems
- Gym plans breaking when life changes
- Sport + gym conflicts
- Soreness/fatigue from real activities
- Uncertainty about what to train next
- Rigid plans not matching real life
- Recovery/load confusion
- Consistency without burnout

## 3. Strong Post Signals

A strong post usually includes:

- A specific real-life situation
- A clear training conflict
- A moment where the plan no longer fits
- Simple human language
- A realization that the issue is the static plan, not the person

## 4. Drift Risks

Reject or flag content that feels like:

- Generic gym motivation
- Generic fitness education
- Bodybuilding tips
- Discipline content
- Productivity advice
- Generic app advertising
- Vague wellness content
- Hard product pitch

## 5. Tone Rules

- Simple Saudi Arabic.
- Clear, complete sentences.
- Normal person explaining a real situation.
- No poetic language.
- No consultant language.
- No therapy language.
- No hype.

## 6. Scoring Rubric

| Dimension               | Points |
|-------------------------|--------|
| Core pain fit           | 30     |
| Real-life specificity   | 20     |
| Metafi differentiation  | 20     |
| Tone fit                | 15     |
| Product restraint       | 15     |

## 7. Score Meaning

| Score    | Verdict      |
|----------|--------------|
| 85–100   | Strong fit   |
| 70–84    | Usable       |
| 50–69    | Needs fix    |
| Below 50 | Reject       |
